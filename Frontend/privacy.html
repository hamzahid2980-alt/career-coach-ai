1. Introduction

Welcome to AI Career Coach ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to helping you advance your professional career using artificial intelligence. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the personal information you provide when you use our platform (the "Service").

By creating an account or using any feature of AI Career Coach, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms described here, please discontinue use of the Service.

This policy is written to comply with applicable privacy laws and to meet the requirements of Google's OAuth 2.0 policies, including transparency around the use of Google Workspace API data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our Service:

a) Account & Profile Information

  • Name and email address — used to identify your account and communicate with you.
  • Google account information (profile name, email, and profile picture) — collected when you sign in using Google OAuth.
  • Career preferences — including target job titles, industries, salary expectations, and work location preferences that you enter in your profile.

b) Career & Professional Information

  • Resume / CV uploads — document files (PDF, DOCX, or plain text) you upload for AI analysis and optimization.
  • Skills, education, and work experience — structured data you enter or that is extracted from your uploaded resume.
  • Skill assessment responses — answers you provide during skill evaluation exercises.
  • Mock interview responses — text or audio responses you submit during AI-powered practice interviews.

c) Google API Data (Only After Explicit User Authorization)

  • Google Calendar data — accessed only after you explicitly grant permission through Google OAuth. We read and write calendar events solely to schedule interview reminders and career milestone notifications you request.
  • Google Tasks data — accessed only after you explicitly grant permission through Google OAuth. We create and manage tasks solely for career-related to-dos and action items you request within the Service.
  • Google Gmail data — accessed only after you explicitly grant permission through Google OAuth. We create drafts of career-related emails (such as professional networking messages or recruiter follow-ups) at your request within the Service.

Google Calendar, Google Tasks, and Gmail permissions are optional and are only requested when you choose to use those specific features. You can revoke these permissions at any time from your Google Account settings.

d) Usage & Technical Data

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information.
  • Pages visited, features used, and time spent on the platform.
  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/region).
  • Cookies and local storage data (see Section 9).

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide the Service: Authenticate your identity, display your profile, and enable all platform features.
  • To power AI features: Analyze your resume, generate personalized career roadmaps, match you to relevant job listings, and provide mock interview feedback.
  • To enable Google integrations: Create calendar events for interview scheduling (with your consent), create task lists (with your consent), and draft career-related emails (with your consent).
  • To communicate with you: Send account-related notifications, product updates, and important security alerts.
  • To improve the platform: Analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to enhance AI models and overall user experience.
  • To ensure security: Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions, abuse, and other illegal activities.
  • To comply with legal obligations: Respond to lawful requests from public authorities when required by applicable law.

We will not use your data for purposes beyond those described in this policy without obtaining your prior consent.

4. Google API Data Usage

AI Career Coach integrates with Google APIs in full compliance with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI Career Coach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Calendar Access

  • Permissions for Google Calendar are only requested after you explicitly choose to connect your calendar within the app.
  • Calendar access is used solely to create, view, and manage interview scheduling events and career-related reminders that you request.
  • We do not read, store, or analyze calendar data unrelated to the interviews or events you schedule through AI Career Coach.
  • We do not share your calendar data with any third party.

Google Tasks Access

  • Permissions for Google Tasks are only requested after you explicitly choose to use the task creation feature within the app.
  • Tasks access is used solely to create and organize career-related tasks and action items that you explicitly request within the Service.
  • We do not read or modify tasks unrelated to those created through AI Career Coach.
  • We do not share your Tasks data with any third party.

Google Gmail Access

  • Permissions for Google Gmail are only requested after you explicitly choose to use the email drafting feature within the app.
  • Gmail access is used solely to draft emails (such as professional networking messages or recruiter follow-ups) at your request. We draft messages as drafts in your mailbox, and do not automatically send them.
  • We do not read, store, or analyze emails in your inbox unrelated to the actions you initiate.
  • We do not share your Gmail data with any third party.

Data Handling Commitments for Google User Data

  • Google user data is never sold to any third party.
  • Google user data is never shared with advertisers or used for advertising purposes.
  • Google user data is never used to train general-purpose AI or machine learning models.
  • Access to Google data is used only to provide the specific functionality you have requested within AI Career Coach.
  • You may revoke our access to your Google data at any time via Google Account Permissions.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard, enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure:

  • Firebase Firestore — A NoSQL cloud database by Google used to store your profile, career preferences, roadmaps, and assessment data. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Firebase Authentication — Used to manage secure sign-in via email/password and Google OAuth. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Firebase Storage — Used to securely store uploaded resume files. Files are access-controlled so only you and authorized services can retrieve them.

We implement the following security measures:

  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only, protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • We conduct regular security reviews and adhere to the principle of least privilege.
  • Firebase Security Rules are configured to ensure users can only access their own data.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users in accordance with applicable law.

6. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data to third parties. We do not monetize your information through advertising networks, data brokers, or any other commercial data-sharing arrangement.

We may share your data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party vendors (such as Google Firebase and AI API providers) strictly to operate and improve the Service. These vendors are bound by contractual obligations to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your data if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: If AI Career Coach is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, we will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With Your Consent: We may share your information with third parties when you have given us explicit consent to do so.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct inaccurate profile information directly within the app settings at any time.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and all associated personal data by navigating to Account Settings → Delete Account, or by contacting us directly.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to Revoke Google Permissions: You may revoke access to your Google Calendar, Google Tasks, or Gmail at any time via your Google Account security settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address provided in Section 11. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

8. Third-Party Services

AI Career Coach integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage) — Firebase Privacy Policy. Used for secure authentication, data storage, and file storage.
  • Google Calendar API — Google Privacy Policy. Used exclusively for interview scheduling and career reminders upon user consent.
  • Google Tasks API — Google Privacy Policy. Used exclusively for creating career-related task lists upon user consent.
  • Google Gmail API — Google Privacy Policy. Used exclusively for drafting career-related emails upon user consent.
  • Google Generative AI / Gemini API — Used to power AI-generated career advice, resume analysis, roadmaps, and mock interview feedback.

We do not embed third-party advertising scripts, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs from advertising networks.

9. Cookies and Analytics

AI Career Coach uses a minimal set of cookies and browser storage to enable core functionality:

  • Authentication Cookies: Firebase Authentication uses secure, HttpOnly session cookies to maintain your logged-in state.
  • Local Storage: We use browser local storage to persist non-sensitive UI preferences and to cache public data for faster load times.
  • Analytics: We may use Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics (anonymized, aggregate data only) to understand how users interact with the platform. You may opt out using standard browser "Do Not Track" settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or sell cookie-based data to third parties.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Notify you via email or through an in-app notification.
  • In cases of significant changes, we may request your renewed consent before continuing to process your data.

Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acknowledgment of the updated policy.
